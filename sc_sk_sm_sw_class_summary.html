6
How You Can Help at Home在家可以怎麼幫忙
Ongoing日常
Take-home帶回家

With this session, all seven S-Blends are complete (sc, sk, sm, sn, sp, st, sw)! No pressure, no drilling — just a few relaxed ways to keep it all fresh: 這堂課結束後，全部七組 S-Blends 都教完了（sc、sk、sm、sn、sp、st、sw）！不用刻意練習，用輕鬆的方式保持印象即可：

  • When reading together, point out any words that start with an s-blend and sound them out together.一起看書時，發現以 s-blend 開頭的字，一起唸出來。
  • Play "I hear a sound" — say a word and have them guess which s-blend it starts with.玩「我聽到什麼音」小遊戲——說一個單字，讓孩子猜是哪個 s-blend 開頭。
  • 5 minutes of flash cards is plenty — keep it short and end on a win.字卡練習五分鐘就夠，保持簡短，並在孩子答對時結束。
